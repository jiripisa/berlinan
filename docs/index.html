Berlinan

A generative MIDI sequencer for macOS in the Berlin School tradition of Tangerine Dream and Klaus Schulze. It runs a small ensemble of sequencer voices that repeat, drift out of phase and earn their changes over time, the way a modular jam does.

It follows the rules of the genre

The engine encodes a style manual distilled from about 225 hours of interviews and breakdowns with Berlin School synthesists. Four of its laws do most of the work.

Repetition earns change
A pattern plays verbatim several times before it may mutate, and every mutation has to earn its place again.
One scale for everyone
All voices share a scale. Transposition moves by scale degree, so the whole ensemble changes harmony together.
Phasing, not randomness
Voices run loops of different lengths and drift against each other. That slow shimmer is the sound of the genre.
Change on the bar
No per-step dice rolls and no swing. Every change is scheduled and lands on a bar line.

It plays your synths

Berlinan is a MIDI instrument, not a DAW. It publishes a virtual MIDI output called Berlinan Out; point Ableton Live tracks or hardware synths at its channels. Audio never passes through it.

INT Berlinan is the clock master.

EXT It follows MIDI clock from Live or a hardware sequencer.

LINK Ableton Link over your network, tempo and bar phase locked both ways.

Made for playing live

Press play and an ensemble starts immediately, from a fresh seed every session. From there, every move is one press and lands on the next bar.

  • Degree buttonsMove the whole ensemble to another scale degree.
  • The B-partA new progression and a new bass line in one press, the classic second section.
  • Arranger autopilotBuilds a piece voice by voice, varies it, returns the theme and ends it. It only advises; you can override any step.
  • Density and ambient modeThin out or fill the whole mix, or slow changes down to about one a minute.
  • Melody extractionPulls a few accented notes out of a sequence and grows them into a lead line.
  • ScenesMorph your synths’ mapped controls toward a named timbre over eight bars.
  • CaptureThe last hour is always recorded. Export bars as a multitrack MIDI file or drag a pattern into a Live clip slot.
  • MIDI learnMap play, degrees, new seed and snapshots to your controller and leave the laptop alone.

Build from source

Berlinan is free software under the GPL 2.0 or later. You need the Xcode command line tools; no Xcode project is involved.

cd berlinan
./run.sh
